# FX and stock data adapters

The workstation has read-only OANDA v20 FX and Alpaca US stock/ETF data adapters alongside Binance spot. Charts, immutable snapshots, dataset capture/import/export and costed SMA research share the existing pipeline. Current daily native research is enabled for AAPL, MSFT and SPY. An explicit Forex daily research extension with price evidence and primary central-bank context is enabled for EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY. FX, stock and ETF screening, reviewed paper entries and asset-specific costs are available. FX saved-breakout replay and qualification are also available. Native research supplies evidence; strategy promotion and automatic FX/stock entries remain separately gated. Separate OANDA practice and Alpaca paper manual order adapters now have connected submission/cancellation evidence; automatic broker entries and complete fill-to-close accounting remain pending. See [demo broker execution](BROKER_DEMO_EXECUTION.md). Provider credentials and feed entitlements are checked separately in Settings.

Select **AAPL**, **MSFT** or **SPY** in the chart, choose **Daily research**, then **Run daily analysis** with a configured AI connection and a ready worker. Results retain the original analyst reports, native rating, request and runtime provenance. Stocks use market, sentiment, news and company fundamentals analysts; ETFs use market, sentiment and news, omitting company fundamentals. The original native stock graph supplies its own research data. Alpaca chart snapshots are linked observations, not injected graph inputs. This is current daily background research, not intraday or point-in-time strategy assessment, and grants no trading authority. The registry definitions and older artifacts remain immutable; current instrument views expose the enabled scope.

For **EUR/USD**, **GBP/USD** or **USD/JPY**, use the same daily research controls after configuring OANDA and an AI connection. Request admission fetches a separate immutable `1d` snapshot, independent of the selected chart timeframe. The extension consumes up to 60 closed daily candles, requires at least 20, checks calendar gaps/freshness/bid-ask integrity, and verifies the snapshot checksum again before using the AI. The snapshot ID, checksum, currency quote convention, calendar, receipt/provider timestamps, closed bars, 20-session midpoint average, five-session change and observed closing spread accompany the saved report. Earlier observations outside the consumed window remain stored but do not determine the window's gap qualification.

New requests use `tauric_forex_daily_v2`, an explicit extension using the pinned TradingAgents 0.5.2 model clients; it does not pass a Forex symbol through the stock graph. Two quick-model reports cover market evidence and policy context, followed by a deep-model risk review, with SDK retries disabled. These stages have no external tools or order access. Earlier `v1` requests remain readable and use their original two-stage price-only workflow. Company fundamentals and exchange-volume assumptions are excluded. The report retains **REVIEW** status and grants no investment rating, strategy approval or trading authority. Scanner escalation can request a daily review; Forex qualification adds a separate structured review bound to the frozen strategy evidence. Chat and autonomous/portfolio research admissions remain blocked. Neither path promotes the REVIEW report to execution authority.

Request admission also collects official central-bank RSS headline metadata and upcoming policy-meeting dates for both currencies. The fixed sources are the [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm), [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/calendars/mgcgc/html/index.en.html), [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/monetary-policy/upcoming-mpc-dates) and [Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmsche_minu/index.htm). The saved context binds source URLs, published dates, source status, receipt timestamps and checksums to the immutable request. The worker verifies that binding and rejects context older than six hours before model use. Each news/calendar source reports available, stale or unavailable independently; a failed source remains visible without inventing observations. News older than 45 days is marked stale. Meeting dates use the source timezone and remain date-only; a multi-day meeting uses its final day.

The report panel and saved report reader show **Policy news & events**, including source links, freshness and partial coverage. The official-bank feed supplies headline metadata and policy meeting dates. New requests also retain an independently sealed **Economic calendar, rates & financing** context. With the subscribed TradingView read-only MCP connection (or optional legacy Trading Economics access), this includes releases from seven days before to fourteen days after receipt, actuals and consensus forecasts when supplied, previous observations and provider metadata (legacy Trading Economics additionally supplies its own forecast and revised fields) and current policy-rate benchmarks for both economies. The nearest 40 releases per economy are retained, with omitted counts and source warnings. Percentage surprises are expressed in percentage points; unavailable or incompatible actual/forecast pairs have no calculated surprise. Date-only releases have no inferred release time or actual. A provider benchmark may represent one facility or one point of a policy range. These are receipt-time observations, not a point-in-time historical database. OANDA supplies current account instrument financing independently when a saved token and account ID are available. Missing, invalid and stale sources remain explicit; no observations are fabricated. Article bodies and positioning are not supplied. A headline alone does not establish a rate change or policy stance. Earlier reports retain their original observations.

The initial registry adds EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY, AAPL, MSFT and SPY. All six existing intervals are supported. Migration `0019_market_adapters` follows the concurrently developed `0018_strategy_qualification`; it adds instrument definitions without rewriting prior instruments, observations or financial history.

## Configure and use

Open **Settings → Market data** in the workstation. Enter the OANDA token and optional v20 **OANDA account ID**, or the Alpaca API key and secret, choose the matching environment and save. The account ID is encrypted with the connection and visible only to the signed-in owner. You can change it while keeping the saved token. Clearing credentials clears the active account ID too. Older saved connections without an account ID remain readable. Alpaca also exposes the feed and 0–15 minute delay. Saved credentials stay encrypted in append-only database revisions using the existing private host encryption key. The browser receives configuration flags and public settings; API tokens and secrets are never returned and their password inputs are cleared after saving. Concurrent edits require reloading the current revision. Changing environment requires replacing or clearing credentials. Migration `0020_market_connections` follows `0019_market_adapters`; the optional account ID needs no additional database migration.

Saving makes no provider request. Choose the symbol in the workspace chart and refresh to check credentials and feed entitlement. **Credentials saved** reports presence, not verified connectivity.

After saving, **Test connection** in either provider form makes a read-only data request using that saved revision: EUR/USD or AAPL at 15 minutes. It verifies OANDA account instrument access when an account ID is supplied, or the selected Alpaca feed/calendar, and checks the returned sample's structure, gaps and freshness. Access failures and data warnings are distinct. The check does not capture chart snapshots, start research or place orders. Save edited fields before testing. A concurrent save or check is rejected rather than silently testing different settings.

Successful checks are timestamped and audited without tokens, secrets or account IDs. The last successful result for the current Settings revision survives reload; a later failed check or new connection revision removes it from the current display while retaining audit history. Host-only revision-zero checks are not reused after reload because the host file can change independently. A sample check establishes access at that time, not ongoing availability or verification of every symbol and historical dataset.

For host-managed setup, `python3 scripts/dev_setup.py` creates the private, ignored `.env.market-data.json` if absent and preserves an existing file. The structure in `deploy/market-data.example.json` supports:

- OANDA: `token`, optional `account_id` and `environment` (`practice` or `live`). With an account ID, refresh first checks the selected symbol through the read-only account instruments endpoint, then requests the historical candles. Without it, the historical candle endpoint needs only a token. Both use fixed HTTPS provider hosts. See the [account instruments API](https://developer.oanda.com/rest-live-v20/account-ep/).
- Alpaca: `key`, `secret`, `environment` (`paper` or `live`), `feed` (`iex` or `sip`) and `delay_minutes` (0–15). Use values supported by the account's market-data entitlement. The empty template uses IEX with a 15-minute delay; this is configuration, not a verified connection.

Development Compose mounts the host file read-only and copies it privately for the service user in the API and portfolio research service. The local launcher sets its path; custom deployments can set `TAURIC_MARKET_CONNECTIONS_FILE`. Each provider uses host configuration only until its first Settings save. Clearing a saved connection disables that provider without reviving host credentials. Preserve the matching `.env.model-key` with database backups; an unreadable saved connection fails closed. Protect host files as credentials. Public provider status never includes tokens, keys or provider error bodies. Its general `connected_verified` flag remains false; the Settings check is a separate timestamped sample result, and each chart snapshot retains its own quality evidence.

Back up before upgrading, rebuild matching services and migrate before starting the API. The shared preview upgrade is coordinated separately with the qualification/autonomy work. No portfolio, cost policy, scanner or autonomous execution policy is enabled by this migration.

In the workspace chart, choose a registered FX, stock or ETF symbol and refresh. Source, feed, session state, quality warnings and tick/share volume semantics are visible. **Research & analytics** saves the selected closed candles and their exact source/calendar context. Choose a saved dataset, enter asset costs and run a replay. Balances, commissions, financing, equity and P&L use the instrument's quote currency: USD for EUR/USD, GBP/USD and the current stocks/ETF; JPY for USD/JPY. No account-currency conversion is implied.

## Forex economic data and paper simulation

In **Settings → Market data → TradingView research**, choose **Connect TradingView**, sign in on TradingView and authorize the read-only connection, then choose **Test economic data**. The subscribed official MCP is the preferred calendar/interest-rate source; Essential or higher is required. No additional Trading Economics subscription or Alpha Vantage key is required. The [official tool catalog](https://www.tradingview.com/mcp/docs) documents cross-country economic calendars and series; beta market data may be delayed, so OANDA remains the Forex price and account-financing source. The app calls only economic-calendar, economic-symbol catalog and economic-data read tools. It discovers rate tickers rather than guessing them, and rejects ambiguous symbols, missing units, unknown scale or stale values. Unverified provider formats produce explicit unavailable sources, never fabricated observations.

OAuth uses the provider’s verified metadata, read-only `mcp:read` scope, PKCE, one-time state and a short-lived HttpOnly callback cookie bound to the original active owner session. The main app session remains SameSite Strict. Tokens and verifiers stay encrypted; code/token values never enter public reports or audit messages. Callback query credentials are removed from the access-log scope and the redirect uses a credential-free status URL. Refresh rotation appends encrypted connection revisions independently of a research rollback. Disconnect disables the connection locally. A separate **Optional paid economic calendar** section retains existing Trading Economics credentials for compatibility; TradingView takes priority when connected, and is the default unconfigured option. Earlier sealed Trading Economics evidence remains readable.

Current account-specific OANDA long/short financing rates and weekday day-count schedules are read through the saved account’s instruments endpoint. The saved report includes receipt time and public instrument fields only; account IDs and tokens are excluded. These signed decimal rates are current quotes, not a history of actual account charges. See [OANDA InstrumentFinancing](https://developer.oanda.com/rest-live-v20/primitives-df/#InstrumentFinancing).

Forex scanners use closed price bars and provider tick activity, with session-aware gap/freshness checks. Daily escalation is deduplicated and does not create a trade. USD paper accounts can simulate EUR/USD and GBP/USD; JPY accounts can simulate USD/JPY. Each deployment requires an explicit fixed financing assumption, including zero. Buys use verified ask quotes; marks and exits use bids. Simulated financing uses fixed opening quote-currency notional, New York 17:00 weekday rollover, and the chosen triple weekday, and is settled on close with ledger receipts. Positive values debit and negative values credit cash. It is intentionally distinct from replay’s preceding-mark basis and does not automatically apply current OANDA rates. Paper simulation is long-only and unlevered; it places no broker orders. Automatic Forex entries remain disabled while daily research is REVIEW; reviewed entries and separately configured attributed exits remain available.

Migration `0021_forex_research_paper` extends provider and paper-account currency constraints. It preserves existing rows and enables no account, deployment, scanner or autonomous policy. Upgrade matching services only after a backup. Preserve the encryption key alongside the backup.

## Calendars and price semantics

OANDA requests unsmoothed midpoint, bid and ask bars together, with daily alignment at 17:00 in `America/New_York`; timestamps are stored as UTC. Root OHLC is midpoint and both executable-side OHLC sets remain attached to every bar. Volume is a provider price-update count, not exchange volume. Completion flags are preserved. The calendar excludes weekends and the documented 16:59–17:05 New York maintenance interval, including DST changes. Daily/four-hour bars retain the provider's alignment. Holiday exceptions are not available in the candle response, so an unexplained absence remains a gap rather than an invented closure. These semantics follow the [OANDA instrument schema](https://github.com/oanda/v20-openapi/blob/master/yaml/separate/v20_instrument.yaml), [candle definitions](https://developer.oanda.com/rest-live-v20/instrument-df/) and [trading hours](https://www.oanda.com/us-en/trading/hours-of-operation/).

Alpaca collections request a bounded dated market calendar and split-adjusted, explicitly selected IEX/SIP bars. Only calendar regular-session bars are retained. One-hour, four-hour and daily bars are built from verified 15-minute constituents, anchored at the actual session open and clipped at its close. This avoids treating premarket/after-hours prices as regular-session daily data. Holidays and early closes come from the saved calendar response. A historical aggregate with missing constituents fails; no prices or volume are filled. Current incomplete aggregates may be omitted until their constituents are available. The [Alpaca calendar](https://alpaca.markets/sdks/python/api_reference/trading/calendar.html) defines dated sessions, while [historical bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars) documents adjustments, feed selection, symbol mapping and pagination.

JSON numbers are decoded with Decimal to preserve precision. OANDA uses the host receipt clock as its declared as-of time; Alpaca uses the host request clock minus the configured delay. Neither response is claimed to provide an independently verified server clock. The selected stock feed's share volume is preserved; IEX is not consolidated volume.

## Data-quality checks

Collection rejects malformed/empty/oversized responses, repeated pagination tokens, wrong symbols or granularities, unordered/duplicate/future timestamps, naive timestamps, invalid prices or volumes, inverted OHLC, crossed/missing FX sides, incorrect calendar periods and old unfinished bars. Stock constituents are checked before aggregation so invalid data cannot be hidden in a larger bar. Non-finite financial numbers are rejected.

Expected trading bars are counted between the first and last observed opens using the asset calendar. Weekend, maintenance and exchange closures are excluded; missing in-session bars remain visible gaps. Recent missing expected bars mark a snapshot outdated even if its HTTP receipt is recent. Zero activity/volume is a warning. Structural verification does not certify economic accuracy, point-in-time availability or execution quality.

Charts can show explicitly flagged gaps/outdated evidence. Dataset capture/import and replay reject internal trading gaps. Forming candles are excluded from captured research datasets. Checksummed snapshot context, sessions, feed, adjustment, quality and receipt/as-of provenance are retained in immutable datasets. Existing continuous crypto datasets remain readable.

An FX/stock import requires `data_context` in addition to the existing instrument, interval, source and candle fields. Declare calendar ID, timezone, price side and volume type. Stock imports require raw/split adjustment plus ordered non-overlapping `sessions` and inclusive `coverage_start`/`coverage_end` containing every bar. The stock calendar IDs are `us_equity` / `America/New_York`; FX uses `oanda_fx` / `America/New_York`. FX may import `midpoint` or `bid_ask`; bid/ask imports must include both sides per bar. Imported calendars and source semantics remain owner-supplied and unverified.

## Research costs and limitations

FX/stock/ETF replay requires `parameters.asset_costs.asset_type` to match the dataset and an explicit `spread_bps`. Existing `fee_bps` and `slippage_bps` remain visible inputs. Additional fields are `commission_per_unit`, `minimum_commission` and `sell_tax_bps`. A fill pays the greater of notional plus per-unit commission and minimum commission; sells additionally pay the configured tax. Sizing reserves conservative commission allowances.

Synthetic full spread is split equally across entry/exit, followed by adverse slippage. Saved FX bid/ask opens/closes supply executable-side prices instead, and additional spread must be zero to prevent double charging. FX saved-breakout exits and stop/target/channel decisions use saved executable bid OHLC when present. The SMA replay retains its opening bid/midpoint approximation for intrabar stops; unseen intrabar spread changes and fill ordering remain unknown.

FX also requires an explicit `financing_daily_bps`, including zero. Positive values debit cash; negative values credit it. Saved rollover events apply at 17:00 New York on weekdays, with a configurable triple weekday (Wednesday by default), using the preceding observed mark without future-bar close information. This is a constant research assumption, not a historical broker financing feed. [OANDA financing documentation](https://www.oanda.com/us-en/trading/financing-fees/) describes actual instrument/rate and holiday-dependent financing; those historical variations remain unverified here.

The experiment remains long-only, unlevered, one-position, quote-currency cash accounting with full-liquidity OHLC fills. Dividends, borrow, shorts, leverage/margin, settlement restrictions, point-in-time universes/delistings, historical corporate-action knowledge, partial fills and latency are not modeled. Funding that exhausts the unlevered cash model rejects the run. Saved results pin parameters, dataset checksums and the engine/cost/calendar source files. Funding receipts reconcile independently into trade P&L and final cash. FX saved-breakout qualification uses the exact frozen dataset, session context, explicit costs, holdout and stress checks. Qualification and native review grant no authority by themselves. Stock saved-breakout qualification remains unavailable; stock daily research and reviewed paper simulation have separate controls. See [stock workflow verification](STOCK_WORKFLOW_VERIFICATION.md).
