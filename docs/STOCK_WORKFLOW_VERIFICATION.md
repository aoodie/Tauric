# Stock screening and reviewed paper workflow

On 4 October 2026, the registered AAPL, MSFT and SPY routes gained calendar-aware stock/ETF screening and reviewed whole-share USD paper execution. Actual connected AAPL and SPY daily research completed through the configured local native worker. Their original **Overweight** ratings, analyst reports and debate fields remain unchanged and confer no paper permission.

## Use the workflow

1. Configure and test the Alpaca data connection in **Settings → Market data**. The selected feed and delay remain explicit.
2. Create a USD paper account and allocation. Save a strategy with a whole-share quantity step, then deploy it to a registered stock or ETF. Enter spread, per-share commission, minimum commission and sell tax assumptions. Account fees and slippage apply in addition.
3. Save a scanner profile with the chosen stock/ETF universe and closed-candle thresholds. **Scan now** records evidence without AI calls. The workspace scanner's **Stocks** filter displays stock and ETF candidates. Repeated observations of the same closed setup reuse its candidate identity.
4. Run current daily native research separately. In **Paper trading**, evaluate the saved rules, attach the matching original report and request its bound OpenClaw review. An actual allowed review permits sizing and execution; an owner test review cannot authorize a stock entry.
5. Inspect the whole-share quantity, costs, risk and attribution before executing a simulated fill. Close the attributed position explicitly, or opt into scheduled monitoring and automatic attributed exits. Pause blocks new entries while enabled exits remain available. Resume is an explicit versioned control change.

Automatic stock entries remain disabled until exact-version strategy assessment is implemented. The retrospective SMA replay is a separate experiment; it does not qualify the saved breakout version or approve a trade. No broker orders are placed by this workflow.

## Prices, costs and safeguards

Paper fills require a fresh, checksum-verified one-minute Alpaca trade series, positive observed volume, a known dated calendar and an open regular US equity session. Delayed data can be screened as clearly identified research evidence but cannot satisfy the fresh paper quote requirement. Forming candles cannot trigger entry/exit rules. Missing recently completed session bars, invalid OHLC, future/stale timestamps, holidays, early closes and unknown calendars block unsupported actions.

The observed trade price is adjusted by the owner-entered half-spread and account slippage assumptions. Percentage fees plus per-share commission are subject to the minimum commission; the explicit sell tax is additional. Sizing uses actual entry and stressed stop-close costs, including nonlinear minimum commissions, and rounds down to the configured whole-share step. Cash, strategy capital, exposure, account risk, shared ancestor allocations and position limits all constrain the result. A stop/target is a trigger, not a promised fill price; exits use a fresh observed quote and configured costs.

Opening and closing records preserve instrument, native original, decision, saved strategy/rules, allocation and immutable quote lineage. Closing receipts also retain stock costs and source/calendar context. Account cash and realized P&L are reconstructed from the immutable journal using Decimal arithmetic.

The route remains long-only and unlevered. Partial fills, order-book liquidity, fractional shares, shorts/borrow, settlement restrictions, dividends, corporate-action position adjustments, delisted or point-in-time universes, latency and broker execution are not modeled. Extended-hours fills are excluded. The selected IEX share volume is retained as selected-feed volume.
