## Persistent data

Data library queues bounded OANDA currency-pair downloads. The application stores complete midpoint/bid/ask candles and provider tick activity in immutable compressed chunks, with source-account/environment fingerprints, requested/actual boundaries, receipt times, gap observations and SHA-256 checksums. These database-backed chunks are included in the normal database backup and restoration. No separate untracked filesystem cache or credentials are passed to an engine.

New requests reuse compatible ranges and download missing ranges only. Inspection and experiments over complete saved coverage make no provider request. Incomplete higher-timeframe tails never become coverage receipts: a later extension fetches the entire newly closed bar. Page boundaries follow the broker's New York calendar, including DST. A history request admits at most 100,000 calendar slots, pages below the provider's 5,000-bar response bound, and has a 64-page recovery limit. Provider faults have three bounded attempts. Missing bars remain gaps; repeated requests cannot erase a gap by relabelling it as a holiday. Different accounts/environments do not silently share coverage. Credential rotation alone does not invalidate prices.

History can exceed an individual study's 5,000-bar limit. Discovery uses at most 4,500 saved bars per study and records its actual chronological cutoffs. Historical downloads are retrospective snapshots; source revisions and original point-in-time availability are not independently verified. OANDA tick activity is not centralized FX volume.

## Automatic research

A saved enabled schedule creates one durable discovery cycle. Cycles pin settings, source checksums and a data cutoff; strategies are recorded before their outcomes. The cycle follows original Qlib/LEAN job identities rather than treating submission as completion. Progress resumes from database state after process restart. Daily hypothesis and engine-job ceilings cover automatic discovery work; other Lab workflows retain their own admission controls. A lower current ceiling blocks new admission, while a cycle never exceeds its original frozen ceiling. UTC accounting is explicit.

Without optional AI, a deterministic bounded rotation explores supported hypotheses and parameter/context combinations. With AI enabled, a saved configured OpenAI-compatible connection proposes structured hypotheses within the same supported family and timeframe schema. Ideas are validated, recorded and evaluated; generated Python or arbitrary orders are never executed. Prior recorded results supply bounded feedback. Unsupported providers/ideas remain visible errors rather than a substitute model call.

A cycle has a frozen 14-day deadline. Transient input failures have at most three recovery attempts across the cycle; permanent input/source failures pause it for review immediately. A resume or new tool call does not reset that recovery counter or the deadline. Daily allowance waits retain the original work, and uncertain model calls retain their reservation without another call. Activity displays the deadline and required review.

AI calls require a positive saved daily USD ceiling, explicit input/output token prices and an output-token bound. Each call reserves a conservative maximum under an owner lock before making the request. Verified usage reconciles the reservation at those saved prices. Missing/malformed usage, transport uncertainty and lost processes retain the reservation and do not automatically retry the request. The control accounts at configured prices; it does not independently verify a provider invoice, surcharge, tokenizer or pricing change. Reducing the current budget constrains new requests. Paid ideas default off with a zero ceiling; rule-based exploration has no model charge.

## Supported research rules

Fixed causal research definitions include moving-average trend, activity-filtered breakout and statistical mean reversion. They are long/flat, quote-currency, unlevered studies. A higher-timeframe filter uses only context bars closed by the signal timestamp. Portfolio signals can combine fixed component weights or switch between trend/breakout and mean reversion according to higher-timeframe context. Aggregate target exposure is capped at one times the experiment allocation. Each fill retains its signal components/reasons; these describe signal attribution, not independent component P&L accounts.

Qlib trains its existing custom single-instrument model. LEAN receives either a matching completed model or the frozen deterministic research plan. Indicators use historical prefixes, test books start flat, orders use the next observed open, and remaining exposure closes at test end. Actual pinned-engine smoke checks exercise a multi-timeframe mixture separately from the existing Qlib handoff and quote-currency/financing workflow.

An earlier expanding-window comparison, baseline screening and stressed-cost jobs are distinct durable records. The final tenth of the saved window is excluded from automatic strategy selection. Closed-trade sample, net return, stressed return and sampled drawdown form numerical screening criteria. A daily-return Deflated Sharpe diagnostic is saved at cycle completion where sample/trial coverage permits; it explicitly does not eliminate dependence or unrecorded-search bias. All attempts and failures remain stored. Inspected test periods are screening evidence. After selecting one frozen candidate, the coordinator registers a separate final confirmation beyond all recorded engine test cutoffs for that instrument, then evaluates it with baseline and stressed costs. Insufficient fresh data remains an explicit rejected confirmation, without a substitute test. A model is trained again with the same frozen training/validation cutoffs for final prediction coverage; final execution is restricted to the reserved range. This detects recorded engine exposure across timeframes, but cannot independently prove that a human or external tool has never inspected prices. Full CPCV remains outside these chronological comparisons; native promotion still requires independently registered qualification evidence.

## Authority and acceptance

Research definitions do not extend the broker's executable strategy schema. A screen-passed research definition is not an exact native qualification, broker approval or deployment. A chosen new family needs its separately tested native execution implementation and the existing qualification/forward gates. No research setting enables broker routes, modifies risk ceilings, activates live trading or rewrites the failed EUR/USD trial. Research replay does not prove intrabar stop protection, live liquidity, AI latency or broker fill parity.

Verification covers reuse/offline reads, incremental data retrieval, partial-bar boundaries, causal context, mixture bounds, idempotency, immutable evidence, owner authentication, cancellation/restart and priced AI reservations. Browser checks inspect separate charts and explanations, save/reload the adjustable budget, and verify desktop and narrow layouts. Hosted CI requires the new browser workflow and three real-runtime workflows; passing synthetic fixtures establishes integration, not alpha. Production acceptance must independently load real OANDA history, prove an equivalent second request needs no provider calls, follow a real automatic cycle to completion, verify backups/source identities and confirm financial authority is unchanged.
