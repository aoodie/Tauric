## Portfolio and research interface

Suggested order: guided research and compact portfolio summary first, then the deeper analysis views as their data and calculations become verified. Extend existing portfolio, research and chat services.

- [ ] **Guided research selector** (image 1): choose a portfolio or instrument, then a research question or supported analysis. Start with market overview, opportunity investigation, instrument comparison and strategy exploration. Show evidence coverage, expected usage where measurable and reasons an analysis is unavailable. Use plain labels rather than subscription tiers or module abbreviations.
- [ ] **Compact portfolio summary** (images 8–11): quick-access panel beside charts and portfolio chat, with allocation, performance and activity views plus research shortcuts. Initially show verified allocations, positions, research status and activity; add value trends when valuation supports them. Images 10 and 11 show the same activity view.
- [ ] **Exposure drill-down** (image 2): clickable composition chart with a readable list alternative and grouping by instrument, asset class, currency, account or strategy. ETF sector look-through requires sourced underlying holdings data.
- [ ] **Performance and benchmark drill-down** (image 4): consistent period controls, verified return calculations, benchmark comparison and clear treatment of deposits and withdrawals. Explain short-period annualisation and distinguish portfolio value changes from investment returns.
- [ ] **FX attribution drill-down** (image 5): reporting-currency comparison and per-position currency effects, using verified valuation and FX history. Keep periods and coverage consistent; define one sign convention for FX contribution or drag.
- [ ] **Investment income view** (image 3): income totals and payment calendar for relevant portfolios. Distinguish confirmed payments from estimates, use payment dates where available and model actual payment frequencies rather than assuming quarterly distributions.
- [ ] **Dashboard and concise explanations** (images 6–7): relevant events, research findings and strategy status, with short interpretations that expand into original reports and supporting evidence. Keep technical setup details in the appropriate settings views.
- [ ] **Responsive layout and visual consistency** (all references): collapse navigation on small screens, stack cards, keep ordinary content within the viewport, reserve monospace for figures and use a restrained colour system. Validate both desktop and mobile use.

Completion requires working data-backed flows and honest empty/unavailable states; screenshot mock data does not count as verified functionality.
